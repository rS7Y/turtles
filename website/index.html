Welcome to

Turtles Restaurant & Bar

Nestled on the pristine sands of Turtle Beach in Morjim, we're an open-air haven where the Arabian Sea meets exceptional cuisine. Our beachfront location offers direct access to one of Goa's most beautiful beaches, known for its nesting Olive Ridley sea turtles.

From morning coffees watching the waves to sunset cocktails with live music, we craft memorable experiences for travelers and locals alike.
